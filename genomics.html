PROJECT 01 / GENOMICS

AI in Genomics

Connecting genomic data, biological interpretation and experimental questions.

Our genomics project is organized around three complementary roles for AI: predicting biological properties from existing data, helping researchers analyze information, and supporting collaborative scientific reasoning.

The aim is to turn complex genomic evidence into clear, testable research questions. Each task begins with a defined endpoint and appropriate data, and progresses through analysis, evaluation and researcher review.

The framework below describes research directions and proposed workflows. It does not represent completed validation or clinical deployment.

Predict

Explore unknown biology using existing data.

  • Variant pathogenicity prediction
  • Splice prediction
  • Protein structure prediction
  • Gene discovery
  • Polygenic risk prediction

Analyze

Help researchers process information more efficiently.

  • Literature synthesis
  • Automated annotation
  • Clinical note abstraction, where appropriately governed
  • Coding assistance
  • Workflow automation

Collaborate

Generate hypotheses and support decisions.

  • Hypothesis generation
  • Experimental design
  • Study optimization
  • Drug target prioritization
  • Scientific reasoning

Application-specific workflows

The tasks below follow the supplied Predict / Analyze / Collaborate framework. Each route describes a proposed research process, rather than a completed result.

Predict

Predict unknown biology from existing data

Variant pathogenicity prediction

  1. Variant & reference sequence
  2. Genomic context & features
  3. Model scoring
  4. Independent evaluation
  5. Prioritized variants

Splice prediction

  1. Reference & alternate sequence
  2. Splice-site context
  3. Splicing model
  4. Compare predicted effects
  5. RNA-based follow-up

Protein structure prediction

  1. Protein sequence
  2. Structure modeling
  3. Confidence & quality review
  4. Functional hypothesis
  5. Experimental validation

Gene discovery

  1. Genomic associations
  2. Functional evidence
  3. Integrate & rank genes
  4. Replication & confounder review
  5. Candidate gene follow-up

Polygenic risk prediction

  1. GWAS summary statistics
  2. Population & LD reference
  3. Build polygenic score
  4. Independent calibration
  5. Research risk estimates

Analyze

Process information faster while preserving traceable evidence

Literature synthesis

  1. Research question
  2. Retrieve primary papers
  3. Extract evidence
  4. Check source support
  5. Evidence synthesis

Automated annotation

  1. Sequence or variant data
  2. Reference & database selection
  3. Generate annotations
  4. Quality & consistency review
  5. Versioned annotation report

Clinical note abstraction

  1. Authorized de-identified notes
  2. Define extraction schema
  3. Extract structured fields
  4. Human accuracy review
  5. Research-ready summary

Coding assistants

  1. Analysis specification
  2. Generate draft code
  3. Review dependencies & logic
  4. Test on known examples
  5. Reproducible script

Workflow automation

  1. Assay & question
  2. Define inputs and steps
  3. Execute workflow
  4. Monitor quality & failures
  5. Traceable analysis outputs

Collaborate

Generate hypotheses and guide research decisions

Hypothesis generation

  1. Observed findings
  2. Literature & mechanism review
  3. Propose explanations
  4. Assess evidence gaps
  5. Testable hypotheses

Experimental design

  1. Scientific hypothesis
  2. Define readouts & controls
  3. Choose units & contrasts
  4. Review feasibility & power
  5. Study protocol

Study optimization

  1. Pilot observations
  2. Identify sources of variance
  3. Compare design options
  4. Check bias & feasibility
  5. Refined study design

Drug target prioritization

  1. Candidate targets
  2. Genetics & functional evidence
  3. Review tractability
  4. Orthogonal validation plan
  5. Prioritized research targets

Scientific reasoning

  1. Scientific question
  2. Evidence & assumptions
  3. Compare alternative explanations
  4. Identify discriminating tests
  5. Revised research question

Research feedback loop

Scientific question→Data & models→Researcher review→Experimental evidence↻Revised question

Predictions remain hypotheses until the relevant independent or experimental validation is complete. Protein structure confidence alone does not establish binding or biological function.

Research outputs

Depending on the task, outputs may include a curated dataset, reproducible analysis, evidence-linked annotations, a prioritized candidate set, or an experimental plan. Reports should distinguish observations, predictions and proposed interpretations.
