AI4Science Learning Resource: Literature Review Checklist
Prepared for the education directory, October 31, 2026.
This is a newly prepared study aid, not a transcript of the online course.

1. Define a focused question and list key concepts and synonyms.
2. Record the databases, search strings, dates and inclusion criteria.
3. Read the original papers. Record DOI, study design, data, methods and limitations.
4. Separate observations from interpretations and model predictions.
5. Compare findings in a table; record differences in methods and conditions.
6. Identify what remains unresolved and explain why your question matters.
7. Check every citation against its source before submitting a draft.
